<h1 class="page-title">Recruitment</h1>

The Covenant prioritizes reliable and compatible members over rapid population growth. Membership is not restricted solely to highly skilled players — activity, cooperation, reliability, and willingness to contribute are equally important. The Covenant maintains a maximum population target of approximately 100 members.

## Stage I — Expression of Interest

A prospective member may request to join the Covenant directly or be approached by an existing member. The initial interaction should establish:

- Their activity level.
- Their primary skills or interests.
- Their previous experience.
- Their preferred playstyle.
- Their reason for joining.

## Stage II — Evaluation

Potential members are evaluated primarily on four criteria.

### Activity

Will they remain active beyond the initial launch period?

### Skill

Do they possess useful abilities in areas such as:

- Building.
- PvP.
- Redstone.
- Economy.
- Resource gathering.
- Leadership.
- Diplomacy.
- Organization.

### Compatibility

Can they cooperate with others and function within an organized community?

### Reliability

Can they be trusted with shared projects and responsibilities?

## Stage III — Admission

Accepted members receive access to Covenant communication channels and community infrastructure. New members should initially be given limited responsibilities while becoming familiar with the Covenant. As trust develops, members may receive greater access, responsibilities, and opportunities for advancement.

## Recruitment Philosophy

The Covenant does not seek members merely to increase its population. Every member should strengthen the Covenant in some way.

The ideal recruit is not necessarily the strongest player. The ideal recruit is someone who remains.
